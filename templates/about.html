About GHANA SEARCH

Welcome to GHANA SEARCH, a worldwide search engine created in Ghana with a vision to make discovering information online simple and accessible.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide users with a simple way to discover information, explore websites, find images, follow news and access useful information from around the world.

Built in Ghana, Made for the World

GHANA SEARCH represents a vision of African technology and innovation. We believe great technology can be created anywhere and serve people everywhere.
