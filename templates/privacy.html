Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Welcome to GHANA SEARCH. We respect your privacy and aim to explain how information is handled when you use our search engine.

1. Information We Process

When you use GHANA SEARCH, the search terms you enter are processed to provide search results. Your selected search region and SafeSearch preference may also be stored in a browser session.

Your recent search history may be stored in your browser session to make searching more convenient.

2. Search Providers

GHANA SEARCH uses third-party search services to retrieve web, image, news and video results. Your search query may be transmitted to those services to provide the results you request. Their own privacy policies may apply.

3. Artificial Intelligence

When you request an AI answer, your question and relevant search information may be sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate a response. Google's privacy terms apply to its processing of that information.

4. Cookies and Sessions

GHANA SEARCH uses browser session technology to remember certain preferences and recent search history. These features help the website operate and improve your experience.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

GHANA SEARCH may use third-party services for website hosting, search results and AI responses. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We may introduce advertising services in the future. If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain relevant advertising technologies, cookies and data practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and information processed through it. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

GHANA SEARCH is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can stop using GHANA SEARCH at any time. You can also clear your browser data and session information through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, please visit our Contact Us page.
